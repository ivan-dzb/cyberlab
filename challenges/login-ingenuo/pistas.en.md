Type a single quote (`'`) in the username field and submit the form. Look at the error message: it shows how the query is built.
---
The query compares the username and password. If you close the username's quote, you control what happens with the rest of the statement. In SQL, `--` turns everything after it on that line into a comment.
---
The administrator user is called `admin`. Try `admin'--` as the username (the password can be anything).
